CHIM Vampire Bridge - changelog
================================

1.0.0
- First release: the vampire features of CHIM OStim Bridge 1.2 as a mod of their own,
  with their own plugin, scripts, MCM and CHIM actions (ExtCmdCOV_*). They work as
  they did there.
- AI actions: Feed_On_Blood, Turn_Into_Vampire_Lord, Enthrall, Release_Thrall, Spare.
- Blood thirst, vampirism by bite (Sanguinare Vampiris), Vampire Lords (Dawnguard's
  own and the names in the MCM), thralls (Vampire Lords, the Volkihar court and the
  names in the MCM), the player's lesser power "Vampire's Thrall".
- Moves over by itself from CHIM OStim Bridge 1.x: bitten, turned and thrall NPCs of
  the save, the vampire state on the CHIM server, the actions and the NPC name lists.
- Works with CHIM OStim Bridge (thralls in scenes) and CHIM ORomance Bridge; none of
  them needs another. With CHIM OStim Bridge a thrall carries an order out in the game
  (a scene with the player or with whoever they name, undressing), not only in words.
- Spare is a standing order: "do not kill the women" holds for this fight and the next
  ones, for enemies who join later too, until the player lifts it; a spared enemy who
  is beaten down is reported to the nearest Vampire Lord, who can bind them at once.
  "Turn the women into thralls": the Vampire Lord binds every one of them who is down.
  MCM: a chance that the order does not hold for an enemy (10% by default).
- Vampire Lords raise the dead: Enthrall (or the player's Vampire's Thrall) on a dead
  person's body brings them back to life, whole, as a thrall (MCM, on by default).
- Fix: a refused action ("there is a fight going on", "she is not here") is answered at
  once, in character, with the reason. Newer CHIM versions no longer pass an action's
  result to the mod, so the agent only learned of it in the next reply. Successful
  actions get no extra reply; every refusal is also written to the server log.
- Notifications and a log file in the game and on the CHIM server (MCM: Debug).
- Does not need OStim Standalone.
- MCM through MCM Helper in 12 languages; the server plugin installs itself.
