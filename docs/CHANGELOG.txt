CHIM Vampire Bridge - changelog
================================

1.0.1
- Fix: Feed_On_Blood, Enthrall, Release_Thrall, Thrall_Task and Spare were often refused as
  "<name> is not here" although that person was right there. The game only knew the exact
  name, while the AI writes names in many ways. Now these all work:
  - you, as "The Narrator", "you", "player", your first name alone, your name with a title or
    a surname, or the name your character has in CHIM's settings when it differs from the game;
  - others the way CHIM lists them to the AI: "Name [Bandit Defender]", "Name [RefID: ...]",
    "Jarl Name", "Name (dead)";
  - a first name alone, or a small misspelling;
  - "me", for the agent itself.
  The server matches every name against the people CHIM sees around you; group words
  ("women", "the dead", "everyone") and tasks stay as they are. The game compares names
  the same loose way. An exact name always wins over a loose one, and the living over the
  dead.
- When a name still matches nobody, the agent is told who is here and may call the action
  once more with the right name, instead of only answering that it did not work. Once per
  agent and minute.

1.0.0
- First release: the vampire features of CHIM OStim Bridge 1.2 as a mod of their own,
  with their own plugin, scripts, MCM and CHIM actions (ExtCmdCOV_*). They work as
  they did there.
- AI actions: Feed_On_Blood, Turn_Into_Vampire_Lord, Enthrall, Release_Thrall, Spare.
- Thrall_Task (MCM, Thralls): thralls at work - follow you, stay or guard a place, serve you
  a meal and a drink, hand over what they carry, or go back to their own day. "Stop" or
  "stay" also ends a walk CHIM sent them on (Travel_To), which used to go on regardless.
- Take_Vampire_Lord_Form (MCM, Vampires): a Vampire Lord NPC takes the winged form and back;
  at your side in a fight they take it by themselves when hard pressed (MCM health) and
  leave it after the fight.
- Blood thirst, vampirism by bite (Sanguinare Vampiris), Vampire Lords (Dawnguard's
  own and the names in the MCM), thralls (Vampire Lords, the Volkihar court and the
  names in the MCM), the player's lesser power "Vampire's Thrall".
- Moves over by itself from CHIM OStim Bridge 1.x: bitten, turned and thrall NPCs of
  the save, the vampire state on the CHIM server, the actions and the NPC name lists.
- Works with CHIM OStim Bridge (thralls in scenes) and CHIM ORomance Bridge; none of
  them needs another. With CHIM OStim Bridge a thrall carries an order out in the game
  (a scene with the player or with whoever they name, undressing), not only in words.
- Spare is a standing order: "do not kill the women" holds for this fight and the next
  ones, for enemies who join later too, until the player lifts it; a spared enemy who
  is beaten down is reported to the nearest Vampire Lord, who can bind them at once.
  "Turn the women into thralls": the Vampire Lord binds every one of them who is down.
  MCM: a chance that the order does not hold for an enemy (10% by default).
- Vampire Lords raise the dead: Enthrall (or the player's Vampire's Thrall) on a dead
  person's body brings them back to life, whole, as a thrall (MCM, on by default).
  The player's power only raises the dead and is given and taken by its MCM switch.
- A thrall raised from the dead lives only by the bond (MCM, Thralls, on by default):
  released, they fall dead again - and with a second switch (on by default) crumble to a
  pile of ash like the game's own raised dead, which can never be raised again.
- Making a sleeping person your thrall is a bite: the game's own feeding animation plays
  before the bond; raising the dead keeps the Vampire Lord's reanimation glow.
- A Vampire Lord player who activates a sleeping person (E, as for feeding) gets
  "Make your thrall" next to Feed (MCM, Thralls; not there unless a Vampire Lord).
- Feeding on thralls (MCM, Thralls): thralls of living blood join Dawnguard's
  DLC1ThrallFaction, so a vampire player gets the game's own Feed next to Talk (E,
  standing); thirsty Vampire Lord companions are told they may drink from them.
- Feed_On_Blood only on living blood: no undead, spirits, summoned beings, vampires
  or children.
- Works with Surrender - Enemies Yield and Comply with Your Demands (optional): enemies
  kneeling in surrender are captives the nearest Vampire Lord hears of and can bind;
  spared ones cannot be finished off by companions while they kneel.
- Fix: a refused action ("there is a fight going on", "she is not here") is answered at
  once, in character, with the reason. Newer CHIM versions no longer pass an action's
  result to the mod, so the agent only learned of it in the next reply. Successful
  actions get no extra reply; every refusal is also written to the server log.
- Notifications and a log file in the game and on the CHIM server (MCM: Debug).
- Does not need OStim Standalone.
- MCM through MCM Helper in 12 languages; the server plugin installs itself.
